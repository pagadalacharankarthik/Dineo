# Dineo Menu: Restaurant Pitch Playbook & Conversion Guide

This guide is designed for sales executives, founders, and onboarding agents to explain **Dineo Menu** to restaurant owners, managers, and staff in a simple, impressive, and high-converting manner.

---

## 🎯 The 30-Second Elevator Pitch
> **The Golden Rule**: Never talk about "databases", "SaaS", or "APIs". Talk about **ink costs**, **table speed**, **reviews**, and **customer smiles**.

### Option A: The "Save Money & Time" Pitch (For Budget-Conscious Owners)
*"Hi [Name], we help cafes stop wasting money printing menus. With Dineo Menu, you get a beautiful, instant-loading digital menu. If a dish is sold out or prices change, you edit it on your phone in 2 seconds instead of printing new cards. Plus, customers scan and leave 5-star Google reviews right from their table."*

### Option B: The "Increase Sales" Pitch (For High-Traffic Cafes/Lounges)
*"Hi [Name], we help cafes speed up tableside service and increase order size. When tables are busy, waiters are slow to bring menus. With our QR stands, customers scan and browse instantly. It loads in under 1 second, displays high-quality photos, and prompts them to order more. On average, restaurants see a 15–20% increase in billing size."*

---

## 📱 The "Show, Don't Tell" Demo (The 2-Minute Hook)
The absolute best way to close a restaurant is to **show them their own menu** running on Dineo Menu.

1. **Pre-Demo Prep (10 minutes before walking in)**:
   * Find their menu online (Zomato, Google Maps photos, or Instagram PDF link).
   * Create a free test account on Dineo Menu.
   * Add 3–5 of their best-selling dishes with prices and images (take screenshot images from their social media).
2. **The Walk-in**:
   * Ask to speak to the Manager or Owner.
   * Say: *"I was browsing your cafe online and noticed you have incredible food, so I set up a quick gift preview for you."*
3. **The Reveal**:
   * Pull out your phone and show their custom menu page: `dineo.in/their-restaurant`.
   * Hand them your phone: *"Tap around. Notice how it loads instantly, no downloading, no pinching to zoom. This is how your customers will see your food."*
4. **The Killer Feature (The Sold Out Demo)**:
   * Open your dashboard on another phone or explain: *"Imagine your chef says the [Special Dish] is sold out. Watch this."*
   * Tap "Sold Out" in the dashboard. Refresh the menu on their phone.
   * Show them the "Sold Out" tag. *"No more awkward waiters explaining after the customer already ordered!"*

## 💬 Objection Handling (Winning the Discussion)

### Objection 1: "We already have a QR code pointing to our PDF menu."
* **The Pitch**: *"PDFs are great for printing, but terrible for mobile screens. To read a PDF, customers have to download a large file, wait for it to load, and pinch and zoom repeatedly just to read a price. If your internet is slow, they give up. Dineo Menu loads instantly, fits perfectly on any phone screen, and lets customers search/filter veg/non-veg in one tap."*

### Objection 2: "Our staff is not tech-savvy. They won't know how to use it."
* **The Pitch**: *"Dineo Menu is as simple as WhatsApp. If your waiters can send a message, they can use Dineo Menu. Toggling a dish 'Sold Out' or editing a price takes exactly two taps. We set up everything for you, and your staff doesn't need to install any apps."*

### Objection 3: "We change our menu frequently."
* **The Pitch**: *"That's exactly why you need Dineo Menu! Every time you print new paper menus, it costs you time and printing fees. With Dineo Menu, edits are free and instant. You can adjust prices, add seasonal dishes, or change descriptions on the fly, and your tableside QR codes update automatically. You print the QR stands once, and they last forever."*

### Objection 4: "Is it expensive?"
* **The Pitch**: *"Dineo Menu pays for itself in week one. Think about how much you spend on design, paper, and printing every year. Think about the orders lost when waiters are busy. We have a Free Trial plan to get you started, and our Pro plan costs less than the price of a single cup of coffee per day."*

## 🚀 The 3-Step "Instant Signup" Script
When the owner is impressed, get them to signup right then and there.

1. **Step 1: Create the Account (2 Minutes)**:
   * Open `dineo.in/register` on their iPad/Phone or your laptop.
   * Ask: *"What is the primary business email you'd like to use?"*
   * Enter their Name, Cafe Name, and Mobile Number. Tap **Create Free Account**.
2. **Step 2: Connect the Menu (1 Minute)**:
   * If they have an existing Excel sheet, PDF, or Zomato link, say: *"Send me your menu PDF/Link via WhatsApp. Our onboarding team will upload all your categories and dishes for you, completely free."*
3. **Step 3: Setup the Table Stands (2 Minutes)**:
   * Go to the **QR Kit** section on their dashboard.
   * Say: *"Let's input your table details. We'll generate a custom high-res poster with your brand colors that you can print immediately. I will mail you 5 premium acrylic table standees with stickers to start your trial today!"*
