# Dineo Menu: Subscription & QR Kit Pricing Plan (Unit Economics & Profit Model)

This document outlines the strategic pricing, manufacturing costs, profit margins, and server infrastructure expenses for **Dineo Menu**. The goal is to establish a pricing structure that covers all operational resources (servers, databases, mailers) while maintaining a high profit margin.

---

## 🖥️ 1. Infrastructure Expenses (Monthly Fixed Resource Costs)

To evaluate your margins, we first lay out the software resources needed to run Dineo Menu at scale:

| Resource / Service | Tier | Monthly Cost (USD) | Monthly Cost (INR) | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 💳 2. Subscription Pricing Tiers (SaaS Revenue)

Since software has almost zero marginal cost per user, subscription plans provide a **95%+ profit margin** once fixed costs are covered.

### 🟢 Plan A: Starter (Free Trial)
* **Price**: ₹0
* **Features**:
  * 1 Dynamic QR Code.
  * Maximum 30 menu items.
  * Limit to **1 active coupon code**.
  * Shows "Powered by Dineo Menu" badge at the bottom.
  * Standard orange color template.
* **Goal**: Act as a lead magnet to get cafes signed up.

### 🟡 Plan B: Pro Plan (Best Value for Standalone Cafes)
* **Price**: **₹999 / month** ($12 / mo) or **₹9,999 / year** ($120 / yr)
* **Features**:
  * Unlimited menu items and categories.
  * Unlimited active coupon codes.
  * Full tableside analytics (Popular categories, views, and scans breakdown).
  * Custom theme colors (Match their restaurant interior).
  * High-res Vector SVG downloads (For custom banner prints).
* **Unit Cost**: ~₹10/month (Server resources).
* **Profit Margin**: **99%**

### 🔴 Plan C: Enterprise Plan (For Multi-Chain Brands)
* **Price**: **₹2,499 / month** ($30 / mo) or **₹24,999 / year** ($300 / yr)
* **Features**:
  * **Remove "Powered by Dineo Menu" branding** (White-label).
  * Connect custom domain (e.g., `menu.their-cafe.com`).
  * Multiple locations management under 1 dashboard.
  * Premium QR Poster standee starter pack (5 free stands).
  * Priority Onboarding (Our staff sets up their menu).
* **Unit Cost**: ~₹100/month (Custom domain configuration + support).
* **Profit Margin**: **96%**

## 📈 4. Breakeven Analysis & Profit Projections

To pay for your monthly hosting and database resources (₹5,110.00 / month), you only need:

* **5 Pro Subscribers** (5 × ₹999 = ₹4,995/mo)
* **OR**
* **3 QR Starter Kit Sales** (3 × ₹2,099 profit = ₹6,297/mo)

Once you cross this small milestone, every new sale is pure profit.
